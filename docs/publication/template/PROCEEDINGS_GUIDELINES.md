*Transcribed from the project guidelines document. Reference copy.*

## The project

Publish a collection of chapters on student research projects alongside
supplementary educational materials. All NSF REU, altREU, MSURE, and tlab summer
interns are invited to contribute. Entirely student-managed through a dedicated
editorial team.

Proceeds from Amazon book sales go to a beneficiary organisation selected by the
participating students. The proceedings are also made freely available on
PDXScholar.

**Last year's proceedings**
- Amazon: `https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FSL57NQ3`
- PDXScholar: `https://archives.pdx.edu/ds/psu/44150`

*(2025 volume: "A Summer of Discovery: How Students Use Computational Modeling to
Benefit Society" — 448 pages, 24 student projects.)*

---

## Why contribute

**Contributors:** a publication for your résumé; a stepping stone to a journal or
conference paper; learn to write a scientific paper, communicate ideas, and
create visualisations.

**Editorial team:** leadership experience; something extra for the résumé; learn
to self-publish a book on Amazon.

## FIGURES AND GRAPHICS

- **EPS format wherever possible.** Use a vector drawing program — Inkscape for
  figures you draw yourself, or matplotlib for Python visualisations.
- **PNG or JPG acceptable for photos.**

*(Practical note: PDF is equally vector and is the native format for pdfLaTeX.
If EPS causes Overleaf compile timeouts, work in PDF and supply EPS only if the
editors require it for the camera-ready zip.)*

---

## CITATIONS AND BIBLIOGRAPHY

- **BibTeX** is the tool and file format: `https://www.bibtex.org`
- The LaTeX template includes a sample `.bib` file.
- Entries can be written by hand in a text editor. Many journal sites let you
  download citation info directly in BibTeX format.
- Easiest route: **Zotero** (`https://www.zotero.org`) — import references from
  websites with one click, then export as BibTeX.
