Capacity is not access.
More beds, or better beds?

What 93 simulations of the September 2020 smoke event say about capacity, placement, and fairness for Multnomah County's unsheltered residents.

Fatima Asghar · Harrisburg University of Science and Technology
NSF REU hosted by Portland State University · mentor Prof. Christof Teuscher

The event, and the people

Thirteen days of smoke. 562.7 µg/m³ at the worst hour.

6,842unsheltered residents (2025 count: 10,526 × >65% unsheltered)
194/312hours above the 55.5 µg/m³ "Unhealthy" line — in two spells

Two spells: a spike at hours 16-21, then 57 clean hours, then the main episode from hour 79. Every value measured by EPA monitors, not modeled.

Four scenarios

Each one exists to answer what the last one measured

A — todaythe real 36 sites, real 2,234 spaces. A measurement, not a plan.
B — one space per personsame sites, every one scaled ×3.0627. Removes scarcity as an explanation.
C — same total, more doorssites grown 1.5×, remainder opens as ten new ~349-space sites. Tests where capacity sits.
D — B + a fairness rule10% of each site (667 spaces) reserved for mobility-limited arrivals. Costs nothing.
Headline access

Who gets inside

B's arithmetic is fixed by design: capacity equals demand, so 578 empty spaces ≡ 550 refused + 28 unreachable — the same number counted twice. The finding is who is outside, not that the numbers match.

What C's edge really is second half = EMERGENCY CUT

Doors, not door-choosing

+306admissions, C over B — and ten random sites from the same pool match it exactly
63%shorter walks than random sites — the optimizer's real contribution

Surplus at the existing sites: +342 spaces (5%) matches C's admissions; +684 (10%) admits every reachable person. C's siting advantage on headcount is worth at most about 342 spaces.

The fairness finding

Scarcity is rationed by walking speed

80% of B's final gap exists one hour after departure. The people left outside are increasingly the people who walk slowest — on every scale we measured.

Scenario D

A fairer door costs nothing

23.7 ptsmobility access gap in B
-0.4 ptsgap in D — at identical total admissions (91.6%)

The gap only exists where capacity ≈ demand — exactly where a county lives. Buy 10% surplus and it dissolves; hold a 10% reserve and it closes for free.

Limitations, in one breath: full information · everyone departs on the early spike · dose depends on the window (24-h B:C ratio is 1.29, not 1.98) · the population is an administrative count treated as all-outdoors-at-once.

The answer

More beds, if you can afford them.
A fairer door, if you can't.

Better-placed beds was the one answer the evidence refused.

Next: the human decision layer — awareness, belongings, pets, imperfect information — specced, registered, and built to close the 1.5–15.6× gap the calibration measured.

B5 · 2020 calibration

The bracket, honestly

Street Roots, Sept 16 2020: ≈90 people at the Convention Center, ≈40 at Charles Jordan — ≈130 of 198 beds. The model fills 198 of 198 at both sites. Because the record is approximate, the honest statement is a bracket: the model over-admits by 1.5–15.6× (1.52× is only the most favourable edge).

That bracket is the measured size of awareness, belongings, pets, and trust — everything the full-information model omits, and exactly what the next phase models.

B6 · freeway fix

2,636 segments, 614.1 km, removed

RLIS freeway classes (incl. the Marquam and Fremont bridge decks — no pedestrian access) were in the walking map. Removed; all 93 runs regenerated. Sheltered counts unchanged to the digit; ~12 people per run reclassified from refused to unreachable (171 network pieces, largest 59,725).

B7 · model card

The regression is a sanity check, not a discovery

Logistic model: P(got inside) from a person's inputs + distance to the nearest site. It re-finds only the mechanisms we built (distance, speed, the COPD decrement) and nothing we didn't (asthma null in every arm). In D, conditional on speed, it re-finds the reserve rule we wrote — with speed-band cells at exactly 100%, its size is unstable and is not quoted. The equity result is marginal: mobility 91.9% vs others 91.5% — the gap is gone.

B11 · the identity

578 = 550 + 28, by construction

Capacity = population ⇒ every empty space is one person outside. Empty spaces (578) must equal refused (550) plus unreachable (28). Informative content: zero. The informative facts: who is outside (the slowest walkers) and where the empty spaces are (the wrong sites).
